Why Prediction Markets Matter

Prediction markets aggregate dispersed information into a single number—the price. Unlike polls or expert opinions, traders have "skin in the game." They're incentivized to be right, not just sound right.

🎯 Accuracy

Studies show prediction markets outperform polls and expert forecasts in elections, sports, and business outcomes.

⚡ Real-Time

Prices update instantly as news breaks. No waiting for the next poll or analyst report.

💰 Incentive-Aligned

Bad predictions cost money. This filters out noise and rewards genuine insight.

The Future

Prediction markets are still early. Regulatory clarity is improving globally, and we're seeing growth in:

  • Corporate forecasting — companies using internal PMs for project timelines and decisions
  • News & information — real-time probability dashboards for elections, geopolitics
  • DeFi integration — blockchain-based markets with transparent settlement
  • AI forecasting — LLMs as market participants, aggregating information at scale
