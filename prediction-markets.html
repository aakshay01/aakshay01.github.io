Why Prediction Markets Matter

Prediction markets aggregate dispersed information into a single number—the price. Unlike polls or expert opinions, traders have "skin in the game." They're incentivized to be right, not just sound right.

  • More Accurate: Studies show they outperform polls and expert forecasts
  • Real-Time: Prices update instantly as news breaks
  • Self-Correcting: Bad predictions cost money, filtering out noise

The Future

Prediction markets are still early. We're seeing growth in:

  • Corporate forecasting: Companies using internal PMs for decisions
  • News dashboards: Real-time probabilities for elections, geopolitics
  • DeFi: Blockchain-based markets with transparent settlement
  • AI integration: LLMs as market participants at scale
