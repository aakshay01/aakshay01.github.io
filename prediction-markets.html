What Are Prediction Markets?

Prediction markets let you trade on the outcomes of future events. Instead of betting against a bookmaker, you trade directly with other people. The price of a contract reflects the crowd's probability estimate for that outcome.

Example: "Will Google have the best AI model by February?" trades at ₹6. That means the market thinks there's a 60% chance. Buy YES at ₹6 → if it happens, you get ₹10 (profit ₹4). If not, you lose your ₹6.

How It Works

  • ₹10 Contract: Every contract settles at ₹10 if true, ₹0 if false
  • Peer-to-Peer: Your YES ₹6 matches someone's NO ₹4 — no bookmaker takes a cut
  • Exit Anytime: Price rises to ₹8? Sell and lock in ₹2 profit without waiting
  • Price = Probability: ₹6 price means 60% implied probability
💡 Key Insight: Prices update in real-time as traders react to news. The market aggregates collective intelligence into a single number.

Skin in the Game

Studies show prediction markets outperform polls and expert forecasts. Why? Traders have money on the line—they're incentivized to be right, not just sound right. Bad predictions cost money, which filters out noise and rewards genuine insight.
