Understanding Prediction Markets

🤖
Tech • AI

Will Google have the best AI model by end of February?

60% chance
YES ₹6 + NO ₹4 = ₹10 Contract
$2M Vol. Ends Feb 28
1

₹10 Contract

Each contract settles at ₹10. Buy YES at ₹6 → If Google wins, you get ₹10 (profit ₹4). If not, you lose ₹6.

2

Peer-to-Peer

Your YES ₹6 matches someone's NO ₹4. No bookmaker - traders trade against each other. Platform just matches orders.

3

Exit Anytime

Bought at ₹6, price rises to ₹8? Sell and lock in ₹2 profit per contract - no need to wait for the event!

💡

Price = Probability

₹6 price means 60% implied probability. Prices update in real-time as traders buy and sell based on new information. The market aggregates the collective wisdom of all participants.
